Mission

At Good Samaritan International Charities, our mission is to provide financial support and scholarships for the children of missionaries around the world. By alleviating the financial burden of higher education, we empower these children to pursue an education, helping them build a brighter future for themselves and their communities.

Vision

Our vision is to see the children of those who sacrifice daily for their communities have equal access to quality education. We aim to foster a generation of leaders who can make a lasting impact, equipped with the knowledge and skills to contribute meaningfully to society. We aspire to extend our reach globally, starting in India, and to expand our support to missionaries in need worldwide.
