Our Work

Scholarships for Higher Education

We provide financial support for bachelor's and master's level education to the children of missionaries.

Focus on Missionary Families

By easing the burden of education costs, we enable missionary families to focus on their calling to serve communities without worrying about their children's futures.

India First, the World Next

Our work begins in India, with plans to expand our reach to missionary families worldwide.

Why Education?

Education is not just a pathway to personal success, it's a foundation for community empowerment and global impact. For missionaries around the world, the financial burden of higher education can be overwhelming. By supporting their children, we help these families secure brighter futures and give the next generation the tools they need to continue making a difference.
