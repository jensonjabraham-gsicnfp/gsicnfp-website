Every gift matters

Your generosity makes a direct impact on the lives of children of missionaries, providing essential scholarships and financial aid to those in need. Every dollar you contribute helps open doors to educational opportunities, creating a brighter future for these deserving students.


As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, all donations are tax-deductible, making it easier than ever to support a meaningful cause while benefiting from tax savings. Your gift, no matter the size, helps ensure that financial barriers don't stand in the way of education for those who serve around the world. Join us in making a difference, donate today!
